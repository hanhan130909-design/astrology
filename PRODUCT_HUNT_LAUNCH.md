## 📋 2. 产品描述

```
Starry Fate is a completely free astrology platform that does both Western astrology (natal chart, transit, compatibility) AND Chinese BaZi (Four Pillars, Day Master, 10-year luck cycles) — in 8 languages.

✨ What makes it different:
• Dual System — Western + Chinese astrology in one place (no site does both for free)
• 8 Languages — English, Chinese, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Malay, Japanese, Korean
• Real Calculations — Swiss Ephemeris accuracy, not random horoscope generators
• AI Deep Reading — Groq Llama 3.3 + DeepSeek powered interpretation
• No Signup Required — Generate your full chart instantly
• Over 190 SEO articles covering zodiac compatibility, BaZi, tarot, and more

🛒 Optional PRO: $3.99 single AI reading, $19.99-$39.99 lifetime reports (Gumroad)

We built this because existing astrology sites are either Western-only, Chinese-only, or locked behind subscriptions. Starry Fate is 100% free for core features, in the languages people actually speak.

Made by a Feng Shui practitioner and TikTok entertainment guild director who wanted authentic astrology accessible to everyone.
```

## 🗣️ 3. Maker's First Comment（最重要！发布后立即发的第一条评论）

```
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Han, a Feng Shui practitioner and TikTok entertainment guild director. I built Starry Fate because I was frustrated that no single platform does BOTH Western astrology and Chinese BaZi — and certainly not for free in 8 languages.

A few things about this project:

🔮 **Why both systems?**
Western astrology tells you your Sun/Moon/Rising. BaZi reads 8 characters from your exact birth time — over a million unique combinations. They complement each other. Most people have never seen their BaZi chart. Starry Fate gives you both.

🌏 **Why 8 languages?**
Because astrology shouldn't be English-only. We have users from the US and Indonesia, and we're building for Southeast Asia. Every page and AI reading works in all 8 languages.

💸 **Why free?**
Core chart generation is compute-intensive (Swiss Ephemeris) but we believe birth chart access should be free. PRO features (deep AI readings, wallpapers) are one-time payments — no subscriptions.

🛠️ **Tech stack:** Next.js, Swiss Ephemeris, Groq Llama 3.3 + DeepSeek, Vercel, Firebase, Resend, Gumroad. Built with Hermes Agent (Nous Research) as my AI coding partner — this whole site was built and iterated conversationally over 3 weeks.

📊 **In the last month, we shipped:**
• 196 SEO articles (programmatic + human-written, 8 languages)
• Weekly horoscope newsletter (auto-sent every Monday via Vercel Cron)
• Social share buttons on every article
• $3.99 micropayment model (validated by industry trend toward spiritual micropayments)
• 6 auspicious calligraphy wallpapers for sale in our Shop

Happy to answer any questions about astrology, BaZi, the tech stack, or building an AI-native product. Ask me anything! 🎴
```
